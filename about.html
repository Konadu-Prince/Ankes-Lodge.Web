About ANKES LODGE

Your home away from home in the heart of Abesim

Our Story

Founded in 2025, ANKES LODGE began with a simple vision: to provide exceptional accommodations in the serene neighborhood of Abesim, Sunyani. What started as a dream has grown into a premier guest house that offers comfort, luxury, and convenience to both business and leisure travelers.

Our journey has been driven by a passion for hospitality and a commitment to excellence. We believe that every guest deserves a memorable experience, which is why we continuously strive to exceed expectations in every aspect of our service.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide exceptional accommodations and services that create lasting memories for our guests, while contributing to the growth of tourism in Abesim, Sunyani.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for travelers seeking comfort, luxury, and authentic Ghanaian hospitality in the Western Region.

❤️

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Hospitality, Sustainability, and Community Engagement form the foundation of everything we do.
