# ANKES LODGE — Hotel & Guest House Website

![Ankes Lodge](https://www.ankeslodge.com/)

> The official web platform for **Ankes Lodge**, a guest house located in Abesim, Sunyani, Ghana.

## 📍 About Ankes Lodge

**Ankes Lodge** is a guest house located in **Abesim, Sunyani, Bono Region, Ghana**, providing accommodation and hospitality services to guests visiting Sunyani for business, leisure, events, and other purposes.

This project is the official website and digital platform for Ankes Lodge. It provides guests with information about the lodge, available accommodation, facilities, contact information, and booking options.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.ankeslodge.com

---

## ✨ Website Features

### 🏨 Accommodation

The website provides visitors with information about the accommodation available at Ankes Lodge, including:

- Room information
- Accommodation details
- Room images
- Facilities and amenities
- Guest experience information
- Availability/booking information

### 📅 Online Booking

Guests can use the website to make enquiries and access booking options.

The lodge also maintains booking availability through external hospitality platforms, including:

- Booking.com
- Airbnb

### 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across different screen sizes:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Android devices
- iPhones and other mobile devices

The interface focuses on providing a simple and accessible experience for guests regardless of the device they use.

### 📞 Contact & Reservations

Guests can contact Ankes Lodge for reservations and enquiries.

**Telephone:** `0544904547`

### 🌐 Online Presence

Ankes Lodge maintains an online presence across multiple digital platforms, helping guests discover the lodge, view updates, and communicate with the business.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of the website are to:

1. Establish a professional online presence for Ankes Lodge.
2. Allow potential guests to discover the lodge online.
3. Present accommodation and hospitality services clearly.
4. Make booking and reservation enquiries easier.
5. Provide guests with reliable contact information.
6. Improve the lodge's visibility through search engines and online platforms.
7. Provide a responsive experience across desktop and mobile devices.
8. Serve as a central digital platform connecting guests with Ankes Lodge.
